Privacy

PaceX privacy policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

PaceX is a running pace calculator and race-planning tool for iPhone and Apple Watch, built by Animesh Sharma. It is designed to work without an account. Almost everything it knows about you stays on your device, and there are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the app.

What stays on your device

PaceX stores your settings and training data locally: your last-used pace and distance, custom race distances, unit and appearance preferences, your VDOT estimate and training zones, and the record of races you finish in Race Mode. This is kept in the app's own storage and in a shared container used by the widgets and the Apple Watch app.

Health data

If you choose "Use a recent run," PaceX asks permission to read your running workouts and walking/running distance from Apple Health. It uses these only to estimate your fitness and suggest training paces, and the values it derives are stored on your device. PaceX never writes to Health, and health data is never transmitted off your device by the app. You can grant or revoke this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → PaceX.

Location

PaceX requests location access "while using the app" for two features. Race Mode uses GPS to measure the distance and pace of a run in progress; the resulting track is used to calculate your result and is not uploaded anywhere. The heat and altitude adjuster uses an approximate location to look up race-day conditions. Location is not collected in the background, and PaceX does not build any location history.

Weather

To show race-day conditions, PaceX sends an approximate location to Apple's WeatherKit service. That request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. PaceX does not send it anywhere else.

iCloud sync

So a new device or a reinstall doesn't lose your training data, PaceX syncs your race history and your VDOT and training-zone settings through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, using your own iCloud account. This data is stored in your iCloud, not on any server run by the developer, and the developer cannot read it. Turning off iCloud Drive for PaceX in iOS Settings stops this sync.

Apple Watch

When you send a pace or race plan to the Apple Watch app, that information is passed between your own iPhone and your own Watch using Apple's device-to-device connectivity. It does not pass through the developer.

Course files

When you import a course GPX file, PaceX reads it from the location you pick, analyses it on your device to build an elevation and pace profile, and does not upload it. The file itself is not copied or retained by the app.

Purchases

PaceX Pro is available as a monthly or annual subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. All purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer receives confirmation of your entitlement but never sees or stores your payment details. Managing or cancelling a subscription is done in your Apple Account settings.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, PaceX schedules a single local reminder before the trial ends. Notifications are generated on your device. There is no push server, and no marketing notifications are ever sent.

Analytics and crash reports

PaceX contains no third-party analytics. If you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide the developer with aggregate, anonymised crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect. These cannot be used to identify you.

What is never done

Your data is never sold, rented, or traded. It is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. There is no user account, no login, and no server operated by the developer that stores your personal information.

Your controls

  • Health, Location and Notification access can each be revoked in iOS Settings.
  • Race history can be cleared inside the app, and clearing it also removes it from iCloud.
  • Training zones can be reset in Settings inside the app.
  • Deleting PaceX removes all data stored on the device. To remove synced data, also turn off iCloud Drive for PaceX or delete the app on every device signed in to your iCloud.

Children

PaceX is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.
