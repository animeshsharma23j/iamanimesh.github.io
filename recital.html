FAQ

Recital Questions, Answered.

Who is Recital for?

Recital is designed for independent music teachers who want one calm place to run their studio.

Can I manage attendance and scheduling?

Yes. Mark lessons, track makeup credits, and reschedule from the week view.

How do lesson notes and practice work?

Keep lesson history close, then send clear practice assignments to families without requiring an account.

Can I keep track of money?

Charges follow attendance, payments take one tap, and monthly statements are ready when you need them.

How does Recital handle privacy?

Recital works offline first with iCloud sync, no ads, and no parent or student accounts.
