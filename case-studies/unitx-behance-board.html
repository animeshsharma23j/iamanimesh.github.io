Overview

Most converter apps solve the math, but still force users through setup. UnitX reframes conversion as a micro-task: express intent first, then edit or save the result.

User Jobs

Conversion needs appear in small, scattered moments. The interface had to support speed, precision, recovery, and repeated use.

TravelerConvert prices quickly without mental math while shopping or moving through a city.
CookAdapt recipe measurements without leaving the cooking flow.
WorkerScan a label, screenshot, tool, or document and turn a visible value into an answer.
Repeat userSave common unit pairs and reopen recent conversions from history or Watch.
