03 / Product suite / Small business

Trade Cloud Apps
BuildX, TradeBill & JobBook

Giving independent trade businesses a clearer path from a job on the books to a bill that gets paid.

Role — Product design & engineeringFocus — Product boundaries, shared workflows, dependable handoffs

Context

Operational work is connected, but individual tasks should stay simple.

A small trade business moves between project context, scheduled work, quotes, invoices, and follow-up. The suite creates a clear home for each task while preserving the continuity between them.

  • Audience Independent trade professionals running day-to-day work from a phone
  • Product challenge Avoid forcing people to juggle disconnected tools or navigate one overloaded all-in-one app
  • Design principle Keep each product focused; make the handoff between products understandable and reversible

Suite roles

Three focused products, one shared operating rhythm.

Each application owns a distinct part of the work. The suite is not presented as a single super-app; it is a system of clear, connected tools.

01

BuildX ↗

Exact construction math and practical tools that support planning and work in the field.

02

JobBook

Jobs, schedule visibility, and follow-up status—so the day’s work remains actionable.

03

TradeBill

Quotes and billing handoffs, designed to make commercial next steps easier to understand.

Design choices

Connected where it matters. Separate where it helps.

The system prioritises recognisable concepts, clear ownership, and visible status over clever cross-product automation.

  • Explicit handoffs A job can lead to a quote or billing task without pretending the transition is invisible.
  • Persistent status Important commercial states stay visible until they are resolved.
  • Shared language Similar labels, hierarchy, and action patterns reduce relearning across the suite.
  • Local confidence Useful work remains understandable even when users are moving quickly between jobs.

Handoff model

A clear next step is more valuable than a hidden integration.

The suite makes responsibility explicit. Each product owns a task, and the person can understand what changed before they continue.

  1. JobBookA scheduled job is visible with its current follow-up state.
  2. BuildXProject context and field tools support the work being planned or completed.
  3. TradeBillA commercial next step begins as a comprehensible quote or bill.
  4. Back to statusThe job’s state remains visible, so nothing depends on memory alone.

Reflection

A suite should feel coordinated, not crowded.

The next stage is to show the suite with approved product screens and validate the handoff model with trade professionals. The quality bar is simple: every product should be useful on its own and more valuable when used together.
