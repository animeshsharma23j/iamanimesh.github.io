Getting started

Let's figure out which ITR form applies to you

Answer four quick questions about your income. No sign-in, no documents — this just points you in the right direction.

Income sources

Do you have salary income?

Income from an employer, shown on a Form 16 — this year, from one job or more.

Capital gains

Did you sell any stocks, mutual funds, or property this year?

New term? Capital gains just means profit from selling an investment — we'll only ask for the detail if you say yes.

Business & freelance

Do you have business or freelance income?

Includes consulting, freelancing, or running a business — not income from a regular employer.

Foreign assets

Do you hold any foreign assets, or earn income from outside India?

Foreign bank accounts, shares, or property — even a small amount counts here.
