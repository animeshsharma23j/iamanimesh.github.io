Step-by-step guide

Here's exactly what happens when you file

Not sure you even need to file this year? You generally do if your income is above the basic exemption limit, or if you had any capital gains, foreign assets, or business/freelance income — even a small amount.

  1. 1
    Sign in with your PAN

    An OTP confirms it's you — no password to remember.

  2. 2
    We auto-fill what's already on record

    Salary, TDS, bank interest, and broker data — pulled from AIS and Form 26AS.

  3. 3
    You confirm what we genuinely can't see

    Capital gains, freelance income, foreign assets — a few plain questions, not a form.

  4. 4
    We compare both tax regimes

    Old vs. new, side by side, with the one that saves you more pointed out.

  5. 5
    You review everything in one place

    Including a check for tax you've already paid by challan, before anything is submitted.

  6. 6
    You verify and you're done

    One more OTP, and that's the whole process.
