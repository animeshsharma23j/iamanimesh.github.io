FAQ

TradeBill Questions, Answered.

Who is TradeBill for?

TradeBill is a quotes and invoices app built for independent trade professionals who need clear, fast quotes and invoices from the field.

Can I build a line-item quote?

Yes. Add service calls, labour, and priced line items, then apply markup and tax to get a total automatically.

Can clients see payment terms and deposits?

Yes. Issue date, validity, payment terms, and an optional deposit request are all part of the quote.

How do I track what's owed?

The home view shows total outstanding, with filters for unpaid and partially paid invoices.

Does TradeBill work with JobBook?

Yes. TradeBill and JobBook are part of the same suite, designed for a clear handoff from a scheduled job to a quote or bill.
