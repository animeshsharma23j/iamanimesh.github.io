FAQ

BuildX Questions, Answered.

Who is BuildX for?

BuildX is for people working with feet, inches, fractions, layout, framing, and material quantities in the field.

Can BuildX handle fractional measurements?

Yes. Exact feet, inches, and fractions are a core part of the calculator rather than an afterthought.

What tools are included?

BuildX brings together calculator, layout, framing, stairs, rafters, area, and material tools.

Can I save calculations to a job?

Yes. Recent-project tools keep takeoffs and useful calculations associated with the job they belong to.

Where can I use BuildX?

BuildX is built for iPhone, with field-ready tools designed around the notation trades already use.
