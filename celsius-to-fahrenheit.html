The formula

Multiply by 9/5, then add 32.

To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and add 32. Water freezes at 0°C (32°F) and boils at 100°C (212°F). Kelvin begins at absolute zero, so it is Celsius plus 273.15.
